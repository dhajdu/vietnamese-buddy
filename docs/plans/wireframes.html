Useful phrases · 12

Tối nay đi ăn hông?
Want to grab dinner tonight?
Casual, warm. hông is the Southern spoken form of không; using it signals you're not reading from a textbook.
Hay là mình đi ăn bún thịt nướng nha?
How about we go for bún thịt nướng?
Hay là softens a suggestion; nha adds friendliness and closes the sentence gently.
Em rảnh hông?
Are you free?
Use em if they're younger or junior; anh/chị if older. Dropping the subject entirely is also natural here.

Grammar · hay là…

hay là + [suggestion] + (nha / đi)?
Suggests an option without pushing. Native speakers reach for it when they'd say "how about…" or "or maybe…".
Hay là coi phim ở nhà?
How about a movie at home?
