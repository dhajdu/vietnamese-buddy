Vietnamese Daily, on ĀRCA

The ĀRCA Wellness colour system applied to the app: cream paper, warm black rooms, and one red for the action that matters. The Vietnamese is set heaviest in Be Vietnam Pro; English sits in Playfair as the caption. A speaker button next to every phrase, word, and card plays it in a Southern woman's voice.

Full spec: docs/brand/DESIGN.md and the live twin docs/brand/design-system.html. Nothing here has been applied to the codebase.

Rule of the system: cream is the paper, warm black is the room, red is the one thing to do. Vietnamese is ink at weight 800 on light and sand on dark, so it is always the heaviest thing in view. Tone tags borrow ĀRCA's status hues (green casual, amber warm, pink playful) and are the only other colour.

Useful phrases 14 · ▶ play all

casual▶

Tối nay em rảnh hông?

Are you free tonight?

In Saigon không softens to hông. Using it instantly makes you sound local.

warm▶

Đi ăn với anh nha?

Come eat with me, okay?

Nha turns a command into a gentle request. Without it this sounds like an order.

playful▶

Hay là mình đi ăn lẩu đi?

How about hot pot?

Hay là opens a soft suggestion; the final đi nudges them to say yes.

Today's pattern
hay là + [suggestion] + (đi / nha)?

Suggests without pushing. Reach for it wherever you'd say "how about…" or "or maybe…".
