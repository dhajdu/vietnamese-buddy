warm

Đi ăn với anh nha?

Come eat with me, okay?

Nha turns a command into a gentle request.

Rules

RuleWhy
One red per viewThe eye always knows what to do next. The streak flame and an eyebrow are the only exceptions.
Vietnamese is ink and heaviest; English is serif and lighterThe language you are learning wins every glance. The translation reads as a caption.
Never bold PlayfairĀRCA's serif voice is regular weight only.
Cream canvas, white cards, warm-black roomsThree surfaces, always in that order of depth.
Pills for actions and chips, 12px for cardsTwo radii keep the surface calm.
Tone and status hues are functional onlyGreen means known, amber means learning, red means act. Never decoration.
One animation: the streak pulseReward the habit, nothing else moves.
